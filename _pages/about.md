Hi! I am a climate scientist with partiular interest in **hydroclimatic extremes (heatwaves, extreme rainfall, floods)**. I use observations/reanalysis, climate models as well as statistics/ML/DL to undertsand these hydroclimate extremes in a changing climate. I am currently a [Postdoctoral Research Fellow](https://www.st-andrews.ac.uk/earth-sciences/people/rj90/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} at the University of St Andrews, United Kingdom.

I earned my PhD in Climate Science from Center for Climate Studies at [Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Bombay](https://www.climate.iitb.ac.in/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}, supported by the **DAAD In-Region Scholarship**. Prior to this, I completed my **Masters in Water Resources Engineering** from Department of Civil Engineering at IIT Bombay, also as a **DAAD Regional Scholar**, and earned my **Bachelors in Civil Engineering** from the University of Kerala under the **Nepal Aid Scholarship** from the Indian Government. My academic journey reflects a progression in scale and complexity - starting from foundational civil engineering, transitioning into hydrology and water resource management, and ultimately expanding into global climate science to understand the factors driving hydroclimatic extremes under climate change.
