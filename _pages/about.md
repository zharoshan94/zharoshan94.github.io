  <p>
    Hi! I am a climate scientist with partiular interest in hydroclimatic extremes
    (heatwaves, extreme rainfall, floods). I use observations/reanalysis, climate models
    as well as statistics/ML/DL to undertsand these hydroclimate extremes in a changing climate.
    I am currently a <a href="https://www.st-andrews.ac.uk/earth-sciences/people/rj90/"
    target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Postdoctoral Research Fellow</a>
    at the University of St Andrews, United Kingdom.
  </p>

  <p>
    I earned my PhD in Climate Science from the
    <a href="https://www.climate.iitb.ac.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">
    Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Bombay</a>, supported by the DAAD In-Region Scholarship.
    Prior to this, I completed my Masters in Water Resources Engineering at IIT Bombay, also as a
    DAAD Regional Scholar, and earned my Bachelors in Civil Engineering from the University of
    Kerala under the Nepal Aid Scholarship from the Indian Government. My academic journey reflects
    a progression in scale and complexity - starting from foundational civil engineering,
    transitioning into hydrology and water resource management, and ultimately expanding into
    global climate science to understand the factors driving hydroclimatic extremes under
    climate change.
  </p>
