Data and method

ECMWF Open Data publishes the source fields 2t and tprate in GRIB2 format. This page uses the browser-readable ECMWF IFS 0.25° feed from Open-Meteo; precipitation rate is calculated from its hourly precipitation increments so the display remains available on this static site.
