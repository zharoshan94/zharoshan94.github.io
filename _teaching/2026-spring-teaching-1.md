I delivered a specialized two-week module as part of the [ES4020 Special Topics in Climate Science](https://www.st-andrews.ac.uk/subjects/modules/catalogue/?meta_modulecode=ES4020) course.

Module Overview
======
Climate change is one of the most urgent scientific problems of our day. This course tackles a variety of topical research subjects to further the understanding of climate dynamics, forcings, and sensitivities, ultimately improving our ability to project future climate change. My teaching contributed to this goal by focusing on the physical mechanisms behind climate sensitivity and the atmospheric dynamics of midlatitude winter weather extremes.

Climate Sensitivity
======
Explored equilibrium climate sensitivity (ECS) and transient climate response (TCR), explaining the different climate feedbacks that dictate how Earth's climate responds to greenhouse gas forcing.

Warmer Climate, Colder Winter
======
Focused on the atmospheric physics and dynamics that question whether a warming global climate can paradoxically drive severe winter cold extremes.
