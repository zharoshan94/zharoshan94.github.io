I contribute to the [ES1001 Understanding Planet Earth](https://www.st-andrews.ac.uk/subjects/modules/catalogue/?meta_modulecode=ES1001) course, delivering lectures specifically focused on the basics of Earth Atmospheric Science.

Module Overview
======
This module introduces the study of Planet Earth, from planetary formation to the present-day processes that control our climate. 

Earth's Atmosphere
======
I teach the fundamentals of atmospheric science, introducing students to the physical processes that govern our atmosphere. These foundational concepts equip students to understand how our global climate has changed in the past and how it will continue to evolve over time.
